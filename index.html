*** # Official Adventist Hymnal – Privacy Policy **Effective date:** May 01, 2026 ### 1) Who we are Official Adventist Hymnal (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile app that provides access to lyrics from the Seventh‑day Adventist Hymnal (1985), streams available hymn audio, and offers an AI-powered study companion called Bible Chat. ### 2) Summary We prioritize your privacy. We do not create user accounts and do not collect traditional personal information (like your name or email). * **Hymnal:** Lyrics, favorites, and playlists are stored locally on your device. * **Audio:** Standard internet logs (e.g., IP address) may be processed by our streaming provider solely to deliver content. * **Bible Chat (Pro):** To verify your subscription and provide AI responses, we process a unique subscription identifier and your chat queries through secure third-party providers. * **No Tracking:** We do not sell your data, track you across other apps, or use advertising identifiers. ### 3) Information we process We do not collect personal data such as your name, email, or location. **On‑device data (stays on your device)** * Favorites and playlists. * Appearance settings (e.g., dark mode, font size, theme). * **Bible Chat History:** Your conversations are stored locally on your device and are not stored on our servers. **Pro Feature Data (Bible Chat)** * **Subscription Verification:** To provide access to Bible Chat, the App sends your App Store `originalTransactionId` to our secure backend (hosted on Cloudflare). This ID is used solely to verify your subscription status with Apple and to enforce fair-usage rate limits. * **Chat Content:** When you ask a question, your message and recent conversation history are sent to our AI inference provider (Fireworks AI) to generate a response. These messages are processed to provide the service and are not used to train AI models. **Network requests** * **Audio:** Requests go to a third‑party music infrastructure provider. Their servers receive standard network information (e.g., IP address) required to route content. * **AI Services:** Requests to our Bible Chat backend include standard network metadata (IP address, device/OS version) required for security and service reliability. ### 4) How we use information * Provide hymn lyrics and audio playback. * Maintain your on‑device settings and favorites. * Verify your Pro subscription status to grant access to Bible Chat. * Generate AI-powered responses for your Bible and hymn-related questions. ### 5) Sharing We do not sell or rent your data. We share information with service providers only as necessary to provide the App’s features: * **Cloudflare:** Hosts our backend for subscription verification and rate limiting. * **Fireworks AI:** Processes Bible Chat queries to generate responses. * **Apple:** Handles all payment processing and subscription management. ### 6) Data retention * **On‑device:** Local data (favorites, chat history) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. * **Subscription Cache:** We may cache your subscription status (linked to your `originalTransactionId`) on our secure backend for up to 24 hours to ensure fast access to Pro features. ### 7) Your choices * **Delete Chat History:** You can clear your conversations within the Bible Chat settings. * **Manage Subscription:** Subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple ID settings. * **App Analytics:** Any crash reporting or system analytics are governed by your global Apple settings (*Settings > Privacy & Security*). ### 8) Children’s privacy The App is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. Bible Chat is intended to be a study tool; however, we do not require any personal identification to use it. ### 9) Security We use industry-standard HTTPS encryption for all communication between the App, our backend, and AI providers. While no transmission is 100% secure, we strive to use reasonable safeguards to protect the integrity of the service. ### 10) International use Your requests for audio or Bible Chat responses may be routed to servers in different countries (primarily the United States) depending on our service providers' infrastructure. ### 11) Changes to this policy If we make material changes to how we handle data, we will update this page and the “Effective date.” ### 12) Contact Questions or concerns? **Email:** josephb401@hotmail.com --- **App Store “App Privacy” disclosure (summary)** * **Data Used to Track You:** None * **Data Linked to You:** None * **Data Not Linked to You:** Identifiers (Transaction ID for subscription verification), Diagnostics (if enabled via Apple).
